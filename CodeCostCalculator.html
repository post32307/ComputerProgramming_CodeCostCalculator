≡Scoring rules and targetsClassroom Java 1.0
Lower is better. You do not need to reduce cost further once it is at or below the target.
Total cost = duplicate cost + complexity cost + size cost
Complexity: sum max(0, complexity − limit) × 2 for each method
Size: sum the ceiling of statements above the limit divided by 5
Duplicates: sum ceiling(tokens per group ÷ 10) × (copies − 1)
What is counted?
if, loops, switch, catch, ternary expressions cost 1 + nesting depth. else ifis counted at the same depth, and &&·||each adds 1. Recursion has no separate cost.
Statement count includes variable declarations, executable statements, and control statements. Block braces, comments, blank lines, method declarations, and forheaders are not counted separately. Constructors and initializer blocks are also analyzed.
